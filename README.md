# ASCII SLAYER

An ASCII-art idle runner in the spirit of *Idle Slayer* — your character runs
forever across a procedurally generated road, fighting monsters, jumping pits,
and grinding gold, XP, and rarer currencies while you plan your next upgrade.

**▶ [Play it here](https://balfik.github.io/ascii-slayer/)** — runs entirely in
your browser, no install, no account. Progress autosaves locally; you can also
export/import a save file to move progress between devices or browsers.

## What's in it

- **Endless run** — auto-fighting, jump timing, pits, combo streaks, loot drops.
- **Town hub** — Forge (enchant/upgrade gear), Shop, Quest Board, Boss Portal,
  World Map, and the Castle Grounds, all reachable on foot (WASD + E to interact).
- **Skill Tree** — five branches (Combat, Speed, Luck, Explorer, Automation),
  including an auto-quest skill that accepts and turns in board quests for you.
- **Castle building** — a 20-stage castle you construct piece by piece with
  gold, wood, stone, iron, crystals and souls; every stage is physically
  visible growing in town and grants a permanent character bonus.
- **Prestige** — reset your run for permanent Prestige Points and a dedicated
  Prestige skill tree.
- **Relics & demonic items** — five unique relics and a pool of demonic gear
  that drop from bosses and the Gauntlet.
- **Boss Portal & Gauntlet** — eight scaling bosses plus an endless
  survive-the-waves Gauntlet mode, both with their own reward curves.
- **A true ending** — max the Castle, collect every relic, and defeat the
  final boss to unlock a one-time celebration screen. The game keeps going
  afterwards — there's no reason to stop grinding.
- **100 achievements**, a full bestiary, milestone tracks, and daily
  login/quest streaks.
- **Public leaderboard** — an auto-generated nickname (no free-text entry,
  by design) and a live global leaderboard with a real-time activity feed
  of other players' milestones, boss kills, and top enchants.
- **Original soundtrack** — synthwave/retrowave tracks composed for the game,
  streamed from [ascii-slayer-music](https://github.com/Balfik/ascii-slayer-music),
  with in-game music/SFX controls.
- **Bilingual UI** — Ukrainian and English, switchable in-game.

## Running it locally

It's a single self-contained `index.html` — no dependencies, no build step.
Clone the repo and open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or just
use the hosted version linked above.
